RECOVERY
Prayer Completion Recovery
Only for a prayer you performed and forgot to record. It is not a missed-prayer tool. The two Fard prayers just before it must already be recorded. Press and hold for three seconds, then confirm.

    
YOUR DATA
Private by default
Prayer history, preferences, themes and location stay in this application's local profile. Nothing is uploaded or synchronised.

    
A BLESSED REMINDER
Qur’an reading
The whole Qur’an is read once each Hijri year: its 2,739 passages in mushaf order, about 7–8 a day, from 1 Muharram to the last day of Dhul Hijjah. A passage counts as read only when you mark it. The reading day changes at midnight in the time zone of your saved location.

Sources and licences
A Blessed Reminder shows the Saheeh International English translation and Muhiuddin Khan’s Bangla translation, stored in the app so it works offline. Its passages follow the 556 ruku’ sections and the juz’ boundaries of the Tanzil Quran Metadata (CC BY 3.0, tanzil.net). The English fonts (Inter, Manrope, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Fraunces, Lato, Nunito, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans, Lora) and Hind Siliguri, Noto Sans Bengali, Noto Serif Bengali, Anek Bangla and Tiro Bangla are under the SIL Open Font License. Kalpurush, SolaimanLipi and Bornomala are OFL; Siyam Rupali and AdorshoLipi are GNU GPL. Li Ador Noirrit is not bundled because its licence forbids redistribution. The Hijri date and the sun and moon positions are worked out on this device with moon-sighting by Aric Camarata (MIT licence) and NASA JPL’s DE442S ephemeris, which is stored in the app; the time zone of the saved location is looked up offline with tz-lookup (CC0). Beyond the polar circles (latitude above 66.56° N or S), where some days have no sunset, the Hijri date is not predicted from the crescent: the Umm al-Qura calendar is used, without the “Predicted by crescent visibility” label.
